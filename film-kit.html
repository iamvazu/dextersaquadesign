Dexter's Aqua Designs · Film kit

Follow the water: eight takes, one flight.

The website plays one continuous camera flight as visitors scroll: from a glowing aquarium in a dark room, through the aquascape, up through a riverbank and a terrarium, out a window to a waterfall, a pond and finally a natural swimming pool. You make it in Google Gemini (Veo 3.1) as eight 8-second clips. Each clip starts on the exact last frame of the one before, so the joins are invisible.

How to make it

The workflow

1 · Still

In Gemini, ask for an image and paste the starting still prompt below. Pick the best one and save it.

2 · Take 1

Choose Video (Veo 3.1), attach the still as the starting image, paste Take 1's prompt, 16:9. Download the clip.

3 · Grab the last frame

Drop the clip into the frame grabber at the bottom of this page. It gives you the final frame as a picture.

4 · Chain

Attach that frame as the starting image for the next take. Repeat through Take 8.

The one rule. Every take starts from the previous take's real last frame, never from a new still. That is what makes the film feel like one camera. If a take ends on a blurry, spinning or sideways frame, generate it again before moving on, because a bad last frame spoils every take after it.

If you use Google Flow (the Veo filmmaking tool in your Google AI plan), you can use Frames to Video for Take 1 and then Extend with each next prompt, which continues from the last frame for you. Download each clip, or the whole joined scene; I can cut a joined scene back into the eight parts.

Optional

A phone-native version

Phones will play the 16:9 film cropped to the centre, which works because every take keeps its subject centred. For a film composed for phones, make a second chain: regenerate the starting still in 9:16, then run the same eight prompts with the aspect set to 9:16, chaining last frames exactly as before. It doubles the generations, so do it after the desktop film is approved.
